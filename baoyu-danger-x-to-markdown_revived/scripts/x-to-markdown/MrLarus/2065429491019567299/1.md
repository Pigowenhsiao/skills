【Extra requirements】:

The poster should feature one original adult character placed in the center or slightly off-center, shown from half-body to knee-up. The character must carry a clear emotional state, such as suppressed sadness, silent pressure, emotional collapse before it happens, lonely defense, frozen memory, deep-sea silence, or quiet anger. The face should be calm but expressive, with tension in the eyes, mouth, shoulders, neck, and hands.

The character wears 【Outfit】 and holds 【Main prop】. The prop should not be decorative only; it must support the emotional theme. For example, an umbrella can suggest sealed memory, a lantern can suggest waiting, a sword can suggest restrained anger, a book can suggest silence, a mask can suggest self-protection, and a glass bottle can suggest trapped words.

Use a large area of deep black, charcoal gray, or near-black negative space as the background. Avoid a realistic detailed scene. Add only subtle paper grain, low-contrast texture, mist, dust, rain, ash, water ripples, or old poster texture.

Build a strong fragmented composition around the character using 【Fragment style】: irregular rectangles, square cutouts, hard-edged slices, fractured panels, broken visual windows, or mirror-like detail fragments. These fragments should feel like emotional cracks, memory pieces, visual noise, or broken space. Some fragments should partially cover the character, while others should zoom in on details such as eyes, hands, hair, clothes, prop structure, flowers, cracks, water, smoke, or light.

Add 【Symbolic elements】 around one side or the lower part of the character. These elements should interact with the character through wrapping, growing, invading, floating, cracking, burning, dissolving, or surrounding. Do not spread them evenly across the whole image. They should feel like the character’s emotion becoming visible.

Use 【Main color palette】 as the base, with one precise 【Accent color / emotional color leak】 appearing only in key areas, such as a scarf, flower petal, light source, crack, eye highlight, weapon reflection, water glow, or burning edge. The accent color should feel controlled and memorable, not overly saturated.

Style: mature anime illustration, dark emotional poster, visual novel cover, character concept art, manga linework, watercolor wash, low saturation, paper grain, asymmetric collage layout, symbolic emotional storytelling, sharp fragments, soft atmosphere, strong cover design.

Avoid: cute chibi style, childish anime face, generic character standing pose, empty expression, 3D render, realistic photo style, messy background, random collage, oversaturated colors, fragments used only as decoration, broken hands, bad anatomy, distorted face, watermark, logo, text.
