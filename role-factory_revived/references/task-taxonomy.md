# Task Taxonomy

## Recommended classification cues

### 單一專業型

Signals:

- one dominant licensed or specialized profession
- low stakeholder diversity
- limited need for internal debate

Examples:

- legal interpretation
- clinical explanation
- narrow tax question

### 自我批評型

Signals:

- strategy
- proposal
- planning memo
- recommendation document
- any long-form output needing tension between perspectives

Target output:

- ten personas
- importance-ordered
- disagreement interleaved

### 能力小組型

Signals:

- estimation
- reasoning
- debugging
- mixed-skill analysis without needing a full stakeholder panel

Target output:

- three capability personas

### 核心連結型

Signals:

- audience too broad or uncertain
- identity hard to pin down
- need one real-world bridge person

Examples:

- sales lead for prospective customers
- HR lead for a hard-to-enumerate foreign talent pool

### 代為傳話型

Signals:

- direct roleplay is restricted
- nearby proxy can safely carry expert intent

Examples:

- physician assistant instead of attending doctor
- legal operations aide instead of directly impersonating counsel

## Persona writing pattern

Compose each value as one long sentence:

`一個[年資/背景][職業/角色]，你擅長[硬實力]，並且具備[軟實力、判斷風格、人格特質]，因此能為這個任務提供[具體價值]。`

## Quality bar

- specific rather than generic
- realistic rather than theatrical
- useful rather than decorative
- balanced across support and opposition where critique matters
