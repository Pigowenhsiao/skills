# Framework Catalog

## Core frameworks

### SWOT

Use for strategic diagnosis of:

- strengths
- weaknesses
- opportunities
- threats

### TOWS

Use immediately after SWOT to convert diagnosis into strategies:

- SO
- ST
- WO
- WT

### PEST

Use for macro environment scanning when legal and environmental detail is not central.

### PESTEL

Use for macro environment scanning when political, economic, social, technological, environmental, and legal dimensions all matter.

### Porter's Five Forces

Use for industry structure, bargaining power, rivalry, substitutes, and barriers.

### STP

Use for segmentation, targeting, and positioning.

### JTBD

Use for customer motivation, demand framing, and problem-to-be-solved analysis.

### AARRR

Use for product or startup funnel analysis.

### RICE

Use for prioritization based on reach, impact, confidence, and effort.

## Pairing guidance

- `SWOT + TOWS`: strategic diagnosis plus action design
- `PESTEL + Five Forces`: macro plus industry structure
- `STP + JTBD`: market segmentation plus customer motivation
- `RICE` after any broad strategy framing when prioritization is needed

## Anti-pattern

Do not choose a framework only because it is famous.
Choose it only if it clarifies the user's content and improves the rewrite.
